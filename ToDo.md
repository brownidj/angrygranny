Features
========
1. Ball movement
   * Very simple - Tea with granny
   * Variable speed
   * Change direction 
2. Ball characteristics
   * Change size
   * Disappear
   * Fade colour
3. Levels
   * _**Tea with Granny**_ Very relaxed. Ball moves at constant speed. Continuous play for up to 5 minutes. Then Granny needs to have a rest. 
   * _**Shopping with Granny**_ Ball changes speed and direction
   * _**Angry Granny**_ Ball reduces in size
   * _**Granny on the Rampage**_
4. League table
   * On phone
   * Online
5. Pause/resume
   * On phone call
   * On SMS
